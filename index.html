I am a Ph.D. student in Wind Engineering at Chongqing University, advised by Prof. Cruz Y. Li. My research focuses on computational fluid dynamics, bluff-body aerodynamics, and particle-laden flows.

Previously, I received my M.Eng. in Wind Engineering from Shijiazhuang Tiedao University, advised by Prof. Qingkuan Liu and Prof. Zhen Zhang, and my B.Eng. in Civil Engineering from Hebei University of Science and Technology.

Research Interest: Vortex shedding, wake transition, turbulent transport, and inertial-particle clustering, void formation, and deposition. I use high-fidelity simulation and Lagrangian particle tracking, and develop reduced-order and data-driven methods for flow reconstruction and aerodynamic prediction.

Research Question: How do coherent wake structures govern unsteady aerodynamic loads and the transport of inertial particles around bluff bodies?

LSTM-based aerodynamic force modeling for unsteady flows around structures

Shijie Liu, Zhen Zhang, Xue Zhou, and Qingkuan Liu.

Wind and Structures, 38(2):147–160, 2024.

pdf | publisher
summary

An LSTM network predicts unsteady aerodynamic force coefficients using URANS data for circular cylinders, square cylinders, and an airfoil. The model is evaluated through interpolation and extrapolation across Reynolds numbers and angles of attack, with mean relative errors below 6%.
