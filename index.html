Shijie Liu

I am a Ph.D. student in Wind Engineering at Chongqing University, advised by Prof. Cruz Y. Li. My research focuses on computational fluid dynamics, bluff-body aerodynamics, and particle-laden flows.

I study how coherent wake structures govern unsteady aerodynamic loads and inertial-particle transport, clustering, void formation, and deposition using high-fidelity simulation and Lagrangian particle tracking. I also develop reduced-order and data-driven methods, including dynamic mode decomposition and LSTM-based models, for flow-field reconstruction and unsteady aerodynamic prediction.

Research

I study particle-laden bluff-body wakes from a mechanism-driven CFD perspective. The goal is to connect flow topology, vortex dynamics, coherent structures, and inertial particle response across Reynolds number, Stokes number, geometry, and inflow condition. My work also covers reduced-order modeling and data-driven unsteady aerodynamics.
