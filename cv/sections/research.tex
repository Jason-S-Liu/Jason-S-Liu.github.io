\cvsection{Research Interests}

\begin{cvparagraph}
  \underline{\texttt{Core research interests:}}
  I focus on \textbf{computational fluid dynamics},
  \textbf{bluff-body aerodynamics}, and \textbf{particle-laden flows}. I study vortex
  shedding, wake transition, turbulent transport, inertial-particle clustering,
  void formation, and deposition using high-fidelity simulation and Lagrangian
  particle tracking. I also develop \textbf{reduced-order and data-driven methods},
  including dynamic mode decomposition and LSTM-based models, for flow-field
  reconstruction and unsteady aerodynamic prediction.

  \vspace{0.7em}
  \underline{\texttt{Core research question:}}
  How do coherent wake structures govern unsteady aerodynamic loads and the
  transport of inertial particles around bluff bodies?
\end{cvparagraph}
